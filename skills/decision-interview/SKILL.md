---

## Method
Research discoverable facts first. For substantial work, establish the desired outcome, material constraints and observable acceptance in a short interview, using answers already supplied. Ask about remaining useful requirements and consequential choices that change behavior, scope, contracts, or acceptance. Present concrete options, a recommendation, and tradeoffs in one concise round where appropriate. When the runtime provides a structured question tool, use it for eligible questions, with a free-text alternative; otherwise ask in ordinary conversation. Respect runtime restrictions on questions and approvals. Continue independent investigation while awaiting answers. Do not ask about routine implementation details or repeat settled answers.

Record decisions, constraints, observable acceptance, and remaining uncertainty. Keep dependent implementation pending while a consequential choice remains open. Once settled, return to the authorized task without a second confirmation unless the user requires one.

## Evidence and stopping
Distinguish evidence from assumptions. Stop when material decisions are settled. Read `../helpers/communication.md` only for ambiguity-sensitive questions or handoffs.
