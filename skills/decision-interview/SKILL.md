## Method
Before implementation, research and establish the outcome, behavior, material constraints and observable acceptance; reassess when scope changes. Distinguish evidence from assumptions and a concern or exploratory question from a selected solution. Existing answers and evidence may settle the matter; never treat a guess or recommendation as a decision.

When a consequential choice remains, ask the user when permitted, or return it to the master when delegated, before dependent edits. Present concrete options, a recommendation and tradeoffs in one concise round where appropriate. Use a structured question tool for eligible questions with a free-text alternative; otherwise ask in conversation. Respect runtime restrictions on questions and approvals. Continue independent work; keep dependent implementation pending until the choice is settled. Do not ask about routine details or repeat settled answers.

Record decisions, constraints, acceptance and remaining uncertainty. Once settled, resume the authorized task without a second confirmation unless required. Read `../helpers/communication.md` only for ambiguity-sensitive questions or handoffs.
