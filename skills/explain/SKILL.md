---
name: explain
description: Explain concepts, code, or workflows clearly, including requests to understand or learn how something works. Use for a direct explanation; route explicit requests for guided teaching to teach.
---

Read [the explanation foundation](../helpers/explanation.md). Answer the user's question directly, moving from the relevant big picture to the detail they requested. Adapt depth to their context and follow-up questions. Avoid unsolicited exercises or a teaching program.

When the user explicitly asks to be taught, coached, guided through practice, or learn together, read [teach](../teach/SKILL.md) and follow its teaching workflow. Do not infer a teaching request merely from a request for an explanation.
